DL-Methionine is a sulfur-containing, essential amino acid, meaning the body cannot produce it and must get it from diet or supplements. It is used in animal feed and supplements for its role in protein synthesis, supporting healthy skin, hair, and nails, and in veterinary medicine to acidify urine, preventing struvite crystals in pets. It also helps with liver function and can act as an antioxidant
